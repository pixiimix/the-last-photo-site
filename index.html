A noir mystery game for iPhone · Support

A woman falls from her penthouse. Your new partner says it was no accident. Search ten locations, collect the evidence and find the truth before six o’clock.

Frequently asked questions

I bought the full case on another device or reinstalled the app.
Open the purchase screen after the Studio and tap Restore purchase. Your purchase is linked to your Apple ID.

Where is my progress?
Your save game is stored on your iPhone. Tap Continue Case on the title screen. Settings › Restart Case starts over.

I can’t find the last clue.
Tap Miller’s ASK button in the room – he points you to the next clue. Some proof is hidden: look closely.

Does the game need the internet?
No. Only purchases and restores go through the App Store.

Contact
