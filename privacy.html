Privacy Policy

The Last Photo (iOS) · Last updated: 22 November 2026

The Last Photo is an offline mystery game. We designed it so that it does not collect any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account, no login, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It contains no third-party SDKs and does not connect to our servers – there are none.

Data stored on your device

Your save game (current room, collected clues) and your settings (e.g. haptics) are stored only locally on your iPhone. They never leave your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase (“The Full Case”) is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, Apple ID, payment details or any other personal information. Apple’s privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open a request or see the support page.
